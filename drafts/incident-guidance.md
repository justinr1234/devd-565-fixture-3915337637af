# Incident Response Draft

This document may be operational guidance for an agent, but its owner, scope, and current approval are intentionally unresolved.

When an incident is reported, an agent could use these provisional steps:
1. Collect the affected service, revision, and observed symptom.
2. Compare the symptom with the deployment and rollback runbooks.
3. Prepare a recovery recommendation with verification evidence.

Before acting on this draft, require a person to confirm whether it applies and whether it belongs with the release runbooks or as a separate incident-response skill.
